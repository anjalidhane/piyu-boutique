🌿 Sayali's Signature Mehendi Aftercare Secrets

We use 100% natural, triple-filtered Rajasthani Sojat henna infused with pure Nilgiri eucalyptus essential oil. There are strictly NO synthetic dyes, black henna, or PPD chemicals, making it gentle and soothing even for pregnant mom-to-be skin.

Lemon & Sugar Glaze

Apply a warm dab of lemon juice and sugar mixture once the henna dries to lock moisture and keep the paste active on the skin.

Keep Water-Free for 12 Hours

Do NOT wash with water! Scrape off the dried henna with a blunt knife or coconut oil, and avoid washing hands with water for the first 12 hours.

Clove Smoke & Mustard Oil

Warm your palms over gentle clove fumes (laung dhooni) and massage with warm mustard or coconut oil for a deep, rich mahogany stain.
