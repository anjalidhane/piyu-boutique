Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a customized fabric painting order take to arrive? +

Because each banner, kurta, and hoop is individually hand-painted and heat-set, crafting takes 2-3 business days. Shipping across Maharashtra takes 2-3 days, and 4-6 days for other states in India. Express courier is available if you have an upcoming date.

Will the fabric paint wash off after a few washes? +

No! We use artist-grade textile pigments cured with heat to create a permanent bond with the natural cotton fibers. Simply hand wash in cold water with mild detergent and iron on the reverse side.

Can I send my own photo for a custom portrait painting? +

Yes, absolutely! You can upload your photograph directly via our Custom Order Form or send it to us on WhatsApp (+91 9309851833). Artisan Sayali will render it artistically in Indian folk or modern portrait style.

Do your Maharashtrian Naths require a nose piercing? +

We offer BOTH! Our clip-on style Naths have a smooth non-allergic comfort clamp that sits securely on non-pierced noses without pain. You can also select the pierced wire option when placing your order.
