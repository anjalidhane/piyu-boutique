✨ Meet The Maker

From a Sacred Passion in Rural Maharashtra to Heartfelt Homes Across India

Namaste! I am Sayali, the founder and artist behind PIHU – Customize Fabric & Handmade Jewellery (affectionately known as Sayali Shoppy in our hometown Kherade Vangi).

Growing up in Maharashtra, our households were always vibrant with celebration — from the divine devotional bhajans of Pandharpur Wari to the resplendent Paithani borders and auspicious pearls of Peshwai weddings. I fell in love with slow, handmade craftsmanship at an early age.

I noticed that in today's fast world, generic plastic gifts and pixelated vinyl prints were replacing true personal warmth. I started painting customized baby shower (Dohale Jevan) fabric backdrops and embroidering milestone shirts for friends. The tearful joy of parents seeing their names immortalized on fabric inspired me to establish PIHU.

Today, whether it is a hand-painted Vitthal kurta, a custom Paalna baby cradle hoop, or an Austrian kundan and Basra-lustre Nath, each piece is conceived individually in our studio.
