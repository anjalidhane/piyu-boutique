🛡️ Fabric Painting Washing & Longevity Guide

Gentle Cold Wash

Hand wash in cold water using mild liquid detergent. Never scrub directly on the painted motif or wring harshly.

Iron on Reverse

Always iron the garment inside-out on medium heat or place a thin cotton handkerchief over the artwork while pressing.

Lifelong Durability

Heat-cured acrylic polymer binders bond permanently into cotton yarn, ensuring the colors remain radiant year after year.
