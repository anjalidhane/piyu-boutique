🪡 The Art of Traditional Aari & Maggam Embroidery

Aari embroidery is an ancient royal needlecraft performed on a stretched wooden frame known as the Khatla. Using an ultra-fine hooked needle (Aari muth), the artisan pulls continuous silk thread loops from beneath the fabric while simultaneously threading pearls, stones, and gold bullion wire (zardosi) on the surface.
